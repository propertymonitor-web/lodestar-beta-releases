# PROJECT MATRIX: notes for the user manual

Collected while testing. Turn these into the manual when it is written.

## Map under the radar
- If the map under the radar is missing, refresh the page (F5). A refresh brought it back in testing. Cause not yet known.
- The small note under the "Show map under radar" tick box says why the map is not showing (no GPS fix, no map chosen on the Live map, or still loading).
- The map needs a GPS fix on the radio, and either an internet connection or an offline map loaded on the Live map.
- The page remembers your map choice per file. A newly downloaded copy, or one opened from a different folder, may start without it.

## Sound
- Browsers keep sound locked until you click or press a key on the page once. Click anywhere after opening the dashboard.
- "Test sound" next to Alert sound plays the alert tones. "Radar ping" (tick box at the top of the radar) pings when the sweep passes a node, only while the radar is on screen.

## Slow or crashing page
- Tick "Lite mode" in the Matrix deck. It turns off the falling code, map tint and map trails.
